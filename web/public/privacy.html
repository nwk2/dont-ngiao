Privacy Policy — Don't Ngiao

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Don't Ngiao is a small expense-splitting app for friends and family. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. The app is run by its owner as a personal, non-commercial project.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address when you register. Your password is stored only as a salted hash — never in plain text.
  • Google sign-in (optional): if you sign in with Google or link a Google account, Google provides your Google user ID, name, email address, and whether your email is verified. This is used only to identify your account and let you sign in. Google's handling of your information is governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Expenses and groups: the groups you create or join, the expenses, splits, and payments you record, and your group memberships. This is the core function of the app.
  • Push notifications (optional): if you enable notifications, your device's push subscription (an endpoint address and encryption keys) is stored so the app can notify you about new, edited, or deleted expenses and settlements in groups you belong to. Turning notifications off deletes the subscription.

How we use your information

Your information is used only to operate the app: to identify you, to show you your groups, expenses, and balances, and to send notifications you have enabled. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or cross-site tracking.

Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers needed to run the app: Cloudflare, which hosts the app and its database, and Google, only when you choose to sign in with Google. No information is shared with other users beyond what the app itself shows to members of your groups (your name and the expenses and payments in those groups).

Data storage and security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, and access to your account is protected by a session token.

Retention and deletion

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists. There is currently no self-service account deletion in the app; to request deletion of your account and its data, contact the owner at the email address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when it is revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact the app owner at ng.wai.kit.22@gmail.com.
